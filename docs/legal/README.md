# Privacy Policy and Terms of Use

The texts the apps show live in `packages/ui_kit/assets/legal/` (one copy, bundled with both apps):

- [privacy-policy.md](../../packages/ui_kit/assets/legal/privacy-policy.md)
- [terms.md](../../packages/ui_kit/assets/legal/terms.md)

When either changes in substance, raise `legalVersion` in `packages/ui_kit/lib/src/legal.dart`: Collect asks again at its next start, and the Console after the next sign-in. Fill in the parts in [square brackets] and confirm both with the PDPO before real data is collected (ADR-026).
