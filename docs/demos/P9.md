## Other P9 work

- **Dashboard:**
  - KPIs: sources tested, % unsafe, open alerts, median time to acknowledge;
  - overdue sources, collector activity, latest tests;
  - filters for area, period and profile, shared with Sources, Collectors and the map.
- **Map (lead's design):**
  - **Sources › Map**, full screen. Glowing dots in the usual map colours (red, tan, green, grey) that cluster when zoomed out and show "name · status" from zoom 13. A dot opens the source.
  - Distance lines from the device to the five nearest points of concern, with locate and layer buttons and the latest-issue callout.
  - **Dashboard card:** the latest issue pulsing, its distance from you, and a link to Sources › Map zoomed on that source.
  - The background covers **all of Uganda** with place and road names: tilemaker vector tiles rendered by tileserver-gl. Abim has an offline pack to zoom 16 (105.6 MB).
- **Sources:**
  - the list with search and filters (all, unsafe, overdue, not tested);
  - the source page: history with flags, trend charts with parameter names and units, photos, sanitary inspections, submitting collectors;
  - stakeholder edits (name, households, working status);
  - "Two changes disagree" cards that resolve sync conflicts (`/v1/conflicts`).
- **Collectors:** built from submissions; a stakeholder can mark a collector known or flagged, and can flag a suspicious submission.
- **Profile page:** contacts, areas, responsibilities, and notification channels (`PUT /v1/me/preferences`).
- **Nationwide areas and the alert chain:**
  - 4 regions, 135 districts and 1,520 sub-counties (OCHA/UBOS);
  - an alert first reaches the lowest level that has someone, then climbs to the levels above that have someone, skipping empty ones, up to national (erratum G16).
- **Sync robustness:** links are checked once per stream, not per page, because an edited parent can arrive after its children. Local errors are recorded as failed syncs.
- **Perf fixture:** a CLI command; its alerts notify nobody and never escalate.
