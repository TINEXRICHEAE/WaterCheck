## 1. These terms

By using the research side of FieldQuire Collect or FieldQuire Console, you agree to these terms and to its **Privacy Policy**. FieldQuire is run by [project lead / organisation] ("we").

## 2. Who may use it

- **Researchers:** anyone may sign up for a research Console account, start projects, build forms and invite a team.
- **Collectors:** anyone may sign up in FieldQuire Collect as a research collector, and fill the forms they are given, join with an access code, or that are open to all.
- Your research account or profile is separate from any account or profile you have on another side of FieldQuire.

## 3. If you run a project

You are responsible for your project and its data. You agree to:

- have the approvals your study needs (for example a research ethics committee and the Uganda National Council for Science and Technology) before collecting data from people;
- ask participants for informed consent, and record it in your forms;
- collect only what your study needs, mark identifying fields as such, and keep participants' identities out of what you share;
- give collectors the training and instructions they need, and look into the visit audit with them fairly;
- choose carefully who may see and copy your forms, and never make records public;
- keep and delete your data as your approved protocol says.

## 4. If you collect data

You agree to:

- fill forms only after the participant agrees, and record their answers truthfully;
- be where the record says you are: each record keeps where and when it was made;
- treat participants with respect, keep what they tell you confidential, and take pictures only when a form asks;
- write respectful messages about the records;
- keep your password, PIN, sign-in codes and recovery codes to yourself.

## 5. Everyone

- Do not try to get into accounts, projects, devices or data that are not yours, or disrupt the service.
- Do not copy or sell other people's information.
- We may hide content that breaks these terms, and suspend or close the accounts, profiles or projects involved.

## 6. Forms and records

- You keep any rights you have in the forms you build and the records you make.
- You allow us to store and process them to run the service, as described in the Privacy Policy.
- A form made public or shared may be copied by others; its records never are.

## 7. The service

FieldQuire works offline and sends records when there is a connection. We try to keep it available and correct, but it is provided "as is", without any guarantee that it will always work or be free of errors. Analyses and indicators are worked out from what was recorded; checking them is the researcher's responsibility.

## 9. Leaving

You can stop using FieldQuire at any time and close your account or delete your profile under **My profile**. A project's records stay with the project.
