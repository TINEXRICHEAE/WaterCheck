# About FieldQuire

> Draft for review. Not legal advice. The project lead confirms this text and fills in the parts in [square brackets].

FieldQuire helps people who work in the field collect data, follow it up and report on it. **FieldQuire Collect** is the app for collecting, also without a connection. **FieldQuire Console** is where data is planned, monitored, analysed and reported on.

FieldQuire is run by [project lead / organisation].

## Sides of FieldQuire

FieldQuire has separate sides for different kinds of work. Each side has its own people, pages, records, **Privacy Policy** and **Terms of Use**:

- **Water testing:** testers check shared drinking-water sources, and stakeholders follow up unsafe water.
- **Research:** researchers build forms and analyse what collectors record in surveys and studies.

More sides may be added later for similar work.

You sign up on one side and accept its documents there. You can later join another side from your account. You then accept that side's documents too, and get a separate, linked account or profile for it. IT admins look after all sides.

## What is the same on every side

- Records are made on devices, kept there until there is a connection, then sent to the FieldQuire server in Microsoft Azure (South Africa North).
- Every record keeps who made it, where and when.
- You can see, correct and delete your own details under **My profile**.
- Contact: [privacy contact: email and phone]. You can also complain to the Personal Data Protection Office, Uganda (https://pdpo.go.ug).

For everything else, read the Privacy Policy and Terms of Use of the side you use.
