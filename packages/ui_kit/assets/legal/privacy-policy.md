## 1. Who is responsible

FieldQuire is run by [project lead / organisation], the data controller. For anything about your information, contact [privacy contact: email and phone].

## 2. What FieldQuire is for

FieldQuire helps communities and districts in Uganda check that shared drinking-water sources are safe. Testers record water tests at sources such as boreholes, springs and taps. District officers and other stakeholders see the results for their areas and act on problems.

## 3. What we collect

### If you test water (FieldQuire Collect)

- **Your profile:**
  - your name and collector type (for example VHT member or student), and your village, parish, sub-county and district;
  - your phone number, email and organisation, which are all optional;
  - whether your phone and email have been confirmed.
- **What you record:**
  - water sources, with their name, type and location;
  - test readings and results;
  - checks of the surroundings;
  - pictures of sources and tests, with where and when each was taken (position, accuracy, height, speed and direction, from GPS); this is written into the picture file too, so a copied picture keeps it;
  - why a test has no picture;
  - messages you write;
  - names you type under "Other";
  - places you add to the map, with your name, which everyone using FieldQuire can see.
- **Where you tested:** the position of your device at each test (from GPS), how accurate it was, its distance from the source, and the reason if you tested away from it.
- **Trips you follow with "Go":** your positions, speed, height and times along the way. They stay on your device: they are never sent to our server, and you can delete them under My trips. Deleting your profile from a device deletes its trips.
- **Your devices:**
  - an ID the device creates when FieldQuire first runs;
  - its type (Android, Windows or web) and, if you give one, its name;
  - when it was last used;
  - which devices you use, and every sign-in with a sign-in code.
- **Your area assignment:** the areas an IT admin, or an assignment code, made you responsible for.

### If you use the FieldQuire Console

- **Your account:**
  - your name, username, organisation and position;
  - your phone and email, and whether they are confirmed;
  - your role, areas and responsibilities.
- **How you use it:** sign-in times, your appearance and alert settings, and what you do in the Console (for example acknowledging an alert or approving a change). These actions are kept in an audit log.

### What we do not collect

- national ID numbers;
- information about households or the people who use a source;
- your contacts, files or other apps.

Please do not take pictures of people.

## 4. Why we use it

We use it to:

- record and check water quality;
- warn the right officials when water may be unsafe, and show what to do;
- show who made each record and where, so results can be trusted and followed up;
- let testers and officials talk about a test, a source or an area;
- keep accounts and devices secure;
- improve the service.

We rely on your consent, and on the public-health purpose of monitoring drinking water.

## 5. Who can see it

- **Stakeholders and IT admins** see the records for their areas: results, locations, pictures and messages, with the tester's name. They can see a tester's phone and email so they can follow up.
- **Other testers** on their own devices see the sources and results for the districts they work in. They see testers' names on messages and on records, but not anyone else's phone or email.
- **IT admins** manage accounts, devices and codes, and can see the audit log.
- We do not sell your information or use it for advertising. We share it outside FieldQuire only where the law requires, or with your district health authorities for public health.

## 6. Where it is kept

- **On your device:**
  - In the Android and Windows apps, FieldQuire keeps its data encrypted.
  - On the web, your browser keeps it unencrypted, so use your own device and a private window only when you must.
  - Your PIN never leaves the device, and neither do the trips you follow with "Go".
- **On our server:** in Microsoft Azure, in the South Africa North region, outside Uganda. Transfers there follow the Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019, and [the safeguards confirmed with PDPO].
- **Maps and routes:** these come from our own server. Your position is only sent to it to draw the map and work out a route (again while you follow a route with "Go"), and it is not stored for that.
- **Map data:** map data © OpenStreetMap contributors. Area boundaries are from the Uganda Bureau of Statistics (via OCHA).

## 7. How long we keep it

- **Water records:** test results, sources, alerts and the messages about them are kept for the life of the project. They are the public-health history of the water sources.
- **When you close your account or delete your profile:**
  - your phone, email and organisation are erased straight away;
  - your name stays on the records you made, so their history still makes sense.
- **Accounts:** accounts unused for 2 years are flagged to the IT admin for review.
- **Security and audit logs:** kept for 1 year.
- **Removed pictures:** marked as removed, and kept for audit only.

## 8. How we protect it

- Connections are encrypted (HTTPS).
- Devices sign every request with their own secret.
- Each person sees only their areas.
- Sign-in codes work once.
- Confirmation codes expire after a day.
- Every change is recorded.
- Backups are encrypted.

If a breach puts your information at risk, we will tell you and PDPO as the law requires.

## 9. Your rights

Under the Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019, you may:

- see the information we hold about you;
- have it corrected;
- object to its use;
- ask for it to be deleted, within the limits in section 7.

You can do much of this yourself under **My profile**: edit your details, confirm or change your phone and email, sign out of a device, and close your account or delete your profile. For anything else, contact [privacy contact].

## 10. Young people

Testers under 18 may take part only with the permission of a parent or guardian, and of their school or coordinator.

## 11. Changes

If this policy changes, its version number changes, and the apps ask you to read and accept it again.

## 12. Complaints

If you are not happy with how we handle your information, contact [privacy contact] first. You can also complain to the Personal Data Protection Office, Uganda (https://pdpo.go.ug).
