# FieldQuire Research: Privacy Policy

Version 1 · Effective: [date of approval]

> Draft for review. This text describes what the research side of FieldQuire does. It is not legal advice. Before real data is collected, the project lead confirms it with the Personal Data Protection Office (PDPO) and fills in the parts in [square brackets].

## 1. Who is responsible

- **Each research project's owner** (the researcher or their institution) is the data controller for the data their project collects: they decide what is asked, why, and for how long it is kept.
- **[project lead / organisation]** runs FieldQuire and processes that data for them, and is the controller for the accounts and profiles of the people who use the research side.
- Contact: [privacy contact: email and phone]. For a project's data, contact the project's owner first.

## 2. What this side is for

Researchers build forms in FieldQuire Console, and collectors fill them in FieldQuire Collect, often without a connection, in surveys, studies and monitoring. Researchers then analyse and export the records.

## 3. What we collect

### If you collect data (FieldQuire Collect)

- **Your research profile:** your name, collector type and area, and your phone number, email and organisation, which are optional; when you accepted these documents.
- **What you record:** the answers to the forms you fill, pictures and other media the forms ask for, the research number of each record, and messages you write about records.
- **Where and when:** your device's position (from GPS) and the time when each record is started and finished, so supervisors can confirm the visit took place.
- **Your devices:** an ID the device creates, its type and name, when it was last used, and every sign-in with a sign-in or recovery code.
- **Forms you may use:** forms given to you, or joined with an access code.

### If you use the Console for research

- **Your account:** your name, email (your sign-in name), organisation, position and phone; when you accepted these documents.
- **Your projects:** the projects you own or help with, your role in each, and the forms, settings and indicators you create.
- **How you use it:** sign-in times, settings, and what you do (for example publishing a form or sending a message), kept in an audit log.

### About participants

The people a form asks about (participants) are not users of FieldQuire. What is recorded about them is decided by each project's forms and its approved research protocol. Researchers mark fields that identify a person (a name, a phone number) as **identifying**: these are left off maps and out of default exports, and records are shown by **research number** instead of a name.

## 4. Why we use it

- to run the projects researchers set up, with the consent each form records from participants;
- to show who made each record, where and when, so the data can be trusted and supervised;
- to let collectors and project teams talk about a record;
- to keep accounts and devices secure, and to improve the service.

## 5. Who can see it

- **The project's team** (its owner, supervisors and analysts) sees the project's records, their points on the map, the collectors' names and contacts, and the messages.
- **A collector** sees the records they made, and the forms they may fill.
- **Other researchers** may see a form a project made public or shared with them, so they can copy it. They never see its records.
- **IT admins** look after accounts, devices and codes, and can see the audit log.
- **People on other sides of FieldQuire** (for example water testing) do not see research records through their accounts.
- We do not sell information or use it for advertising. We share it outside FieldQuire only where the law requires.

## 6. Where it is kept

- **On your device:** encrypted in the Android and Windows apps; unencrypted in a web browser, so use your own device.
- **On our server:** in Microsoft Azure, South Africa North, outside Uganda. Transfers there follow the Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019, and [the safeguards confirmed with PDPO].
- **Maps:** your position is sent to our map server only to draw the map, and is not stored for that.

## 7. How long we keep it

- **Project records:** for as long as the project's owner decides under their approved protocol, then deleted or archived as they ask. [Default retention to be confirmed.]
- **When you close your account or delete your profile:** your phone, email and organisation are erased straight away; your name stays on the records you made.
- **Security and audit logs:** kept for 1 year.

## 8. How we protect it

Encrypted connections (HTTPS), devices that sign every request, one-time sign-in and recovery codes, access limited to each project's team, a record of every change, and encrypted backups. If a breach puts information at risk, we will tell those affected and PDPO as the law requires.

## 9. Your rights

Under the Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019, you may see, correct, object to the use of, and ask to delete information about you. You can do much of this under **My profile**. Participants should contact the project's owner, whose contact the collector gives them.

## 10. Changes

If this policy changes, its version number changes, and the apps ask you once to read and accept it again.

## 11. Complaints

Contact [privacy contact] first. You can also complain to the Personal Data Protection Office, Uganda (https://pdpo.go.ug).
